Mine together below the 0.1 minimum. Small holders fund one pool address; the pool locks ≥ 0.1, claims every round at exactly its fair share, and splits the proceeds pro-rata — with accounting anyone can recompute.

How to join

  1. Use the calculator below: enter your own RESET address and a planned amount (a multiple of 0.01). Your exact transfer amount is computed locally in your browser — no registration, no GitHub account. Your calculation is recorded in the pool's registry for reconciliation.
  2. From your own RESET wallet, transfer exactly that amount to the pool address — every digit matters; do not round it and do not improvise a different amount. You pay your own TNK transfer fee.
  3. Wait about a minute. The pool watches its balance and matches the amount's tail to your address automatically — the tail is your address's fingerprint. The full amount is credited as your contribution.
  4. Check the Ledger tab (or look your address up on the Overview tab). Your share of every round = your contribution ÷ pool total, snapshotted per round. Earnings accumulate and are paid out once they reach 0.01.

Your exact transfer amount — self-service calculator

Your address has a deterministic fingerprint: a tail of 1–999999 units derived from the address itself (sha256). planned (a 0.01 multiple) + tail = an amount that is uniquely yours — that is how the pool credits you automatically.

⚠ Before you transfer
Only ever transfer the exact amount the calculator gives you. A transfer with a wrong or rounded tail cannot be matched automatically and is parked as unclaimed. Claiming it needs no GitHub account and no signature: the amount's tail is checked against the address it belongs to — if they match, it is credited (ask the operator to run the claim, or see the README). A brand-new address that appears nowhere on the network yet may also land in unclaimed first — same claim path.

Rules — the honest version

  • Fee: 0. Claim level: always exactly 1000 (fair share, never over — no strikes, no penalties).
  • Pooling grants access, not extra yield. The Mine pays linearly in deposit; the pool earns exactly what its combined deposit would earn solo.
  • Exits take about a day: leaving requires unlock plus the contract's 144-Bitcoin-block exit wait, then withdraw. No instant exits.
  • Accounting is provable; custody is trusted. RESET has no pool/multisig primitive, so the operator's key controls the funds. Every contribution, claim and payout is published and hash-chained so any cheating would be visible and attributable — but it cannot be made impossible. This is an experiment, not an investment; no returns are promised.

Experience Shares — the proposed newcomer program

Proposed to the team — applications open once the team stakes Read the proposal (issue #3)

The RESET team is invited to stake in this pool like any other member. The yield from the team's stake would then be allocated to newcomers as mining-experience shares:

  1. Apply with your GitHub account. Claims are made from your own authenticated GitHub account, so identity can't be spoofed, and each account is checked automatically for account age and real activity. One claim per GitHub account, ever.
  2. Register your RESET address. The applicant registers the address the experience shares belong to.
  3. The yield is credited in the pool, under your address. Your share of the team's yield is recorded on the public ledger from the first round. The allocation never leaves the pool's accounting — no funds pass through anyone's hands.
  4. It's yours: withdraw it, or let it compound. Each round you earn pro-rata on top of it, and you can either withdraw your earnings under the pool's normal payout rules, or keep them in and compound your stake.

Anti-Sybil rules

  • One claim per GitHub account, ever — accounts are checked automatically for age and real activity.
  • Contribution first: claimants run the signer and post headers for a while before claiming — visible on the network, and hard to fake in bulk.
  • Claims are capped per period, so the program grows at a controlled pace.
  • The full claim list is published — duplicate or fake claims are obvious to everyone.

To be clear: nothing is asked for the operator. The team's principal stays the team's own share throughout — only its yield is given away, and every movement is publicly verifiable. And as with everything in this pool: pooling grants access, not extra yield, and custody is trusted — see the rules above.

Verify it yourself

Each round report publishes its full inputs: the contributor snapshot, the claim amount (match it against the chain's claim record for the pool address and round), the fee and every share. Recompute:

sharei = (claim − fee + carried dust) × depositi ÷ total deposits

Integer-division dust is carried into the next round, never kept. At any time: Σ claims = Σ pending + Σ paid + fees + carried dust. An open-source verifier that recomputes reports independently is available from the operator — altering a single share makes verification fail.
