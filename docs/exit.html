Exiting, in four lines

  • A position on the ledger under your address can be exited — a right, not an approval.
  • Full exits only: principal + pending earnings, settled in one payment.
  • Paid only to the address you contributed from — no step can change the payee.
  • The pool pays the transfer fee; you receive the full amount.

The ten steps

  1. 1
    Check your position. Enter your address on the Overview or Ledger tab — that is what an exit settles.
  2. 2
    File an intent. The registry issues a one-time statement (nonce, 30 min). Your own AI can file it.
  3. 3
    Sign it. Sign the exact text with your own signer — no transfer, zero balance OK.
  4. 4
    Submit. The nonce burns on first use — replays fail. Verified signature = case opened.
  5. 5
    Amount frozen. Your payout is fixed from the ledger at the moment the case opens.
  6. 6
    Pool unlocks. Minimum 0.1 per unlock (contract); the excess returns after the wait and re-locks.
  7. 7
    Wait. 144 Bitcoin blocks — about 24 hours.
  8. 8
    Pool withdraws. At the first window after maturity, the amount returns to the pool balance.
  9. 9
    You are paid. The full frozen amount goes to your original address.
  10. 10
    Closed on the ledger. Hash-chained events record the exit — anyone can recompute it.

The honest frictions

  • While unlocked, that part earns nothing — those rounds pay everyone less, pro-rata, including you.
  • If a pool claim is still unsettled, the contract refuses the withdraw — pickup slips to the next window.
  • If an exit leaves under 0.1 staked, the rest sits idle until the balance tops it up to 0.1 again.
