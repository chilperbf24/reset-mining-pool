Exiting is your right, not a favor: a verified request is executed automatically. Three things to know — exits are full only (principal + pending earnings, settled in one payment); payout takes about 24 hours (the contract's 144-block exit wait); and if an exit would leave the pool's deposit below 0.1, the whole pool is dissolved and everyone is settled instead.

No transfer is needed to prove it is you — a signature is. Your coins are inside the pool, so your wallet may be empty; signing costs nothing and works at zero balance. The server issues a one-time statement (the official signed-message format); you sign it with your own signer and submit the signature. Anyone — including your own AI — can file the request and carry the submission, but only your key can sign. And the payout can only ever go to your own ledger address: no step in this flow can name a different payee.
